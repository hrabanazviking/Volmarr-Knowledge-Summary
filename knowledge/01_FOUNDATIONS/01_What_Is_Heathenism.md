# Heathenism and Norse Paganism

## In Brief

The ancestral pre-Christian worldview of Northern Europe, revived as a living, nature-centered polytheistic tradition honoring Gods, ancestors, and wights.

## Core Idea

In the Heathen Third Path corpus, **Heathenism and Norse Paganism** serves as a vital structural pillar.

The corpus synthesizes this idea across 117 independent articles, drawing upon perspectives from Astrid Freyjasdottir, Caducea, Volmarr.

## Distinctions

- **Living Tradition vs Academic Antiquarianism:** Heathenism is a lived religion, not merely an intellectual study of dead lore.
- **Pluralism vs Dogmatism:** Embraces diverse expressions (Asatru, Vanatru, Norse-Wicca) without enforcing central dogmatic orthodoxy.
