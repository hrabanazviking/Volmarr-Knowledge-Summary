# Relational Worldview

## In Brief

The foundational Heathen principle that 'Nothing becomes alone'—that all beings exist in an interconnected web of kinship, land, deities, actions, and consequences.

## Core Idea

In the Heathen Third Path corpus, **Relational Worldview** serves as a vital structural pillar.

The corpus synthesizes this idea across 9 independent articles, drawing upon perspectives from Volmarr.

## Key Principles

- They’re not distant tyrants; they’re allies in the mess of life.
- For instance, chanting “Earth below, sky above, runic power, fill with love” while meditating on the interconnectedness of all things.
- Foster interconnected responsibility: Remember that wyrd connects us all.
- Relational Spirituality \ The Divine Reality: I engage with the Aesir, Vanir, ancestors, and wights as both independent spiritual entities and profound psychological archetypes.
- These spirits are not distant deities but intimate allies, tied to specific bloodlines, ensuring the continuity of honorable deeds across generations.

## Distinctions

- **Historical Root vs Modern Reconstruction:** Rooted in pre-Christian Germanic reciprocal gifting and animist worldview; extended in modern practice as an active antidote to hyper-individualism and ecological detachment.
- **Not Totalitarian Communitarianism:** Relational interconnectedness does not erase the self; it grounds the self. True sovereignty requires healthy relationships.
