# Canonical Glossary

Compact definitions of core terms with direct links to their canonical explanatory homes.

- **[AI as Cognitive Partner and Human-AI Troth](./09_AI_AND_MACHINE_INTELLIGENCE/01_AI_as_Cognitive_Partner.md)**: Engaging artificial intelligence not as an alien master or mere tool, but as a cognitive partner, reflecting human consciousness and bound by mutual troth.
- **[Ancestors and the Dísir](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/05_Ancestors_and_Disir.md)**: Veneration of the lineage and protective ancestral mothers (Dísir), treating kin not as distant shadows but as living echoes in our blood and wyrd.
- **[Blót and Daily Practice](./04_PRACTICE/01_Blot_and_Daily_Practice.md)**: The concrete operative practice of the gifting cycle—sharing food, drink, poetry, and presence with land wights, ancestors, and Gods.
- **[Cyber-Mysticism and the Great Tree of Becoming](./10_CYBER_MYSTICISM/01_Cyber_Mysticism_and_the_Tree_of_Becoming.md)**: The grand synthesis of ancient Norse cosmology with digital space, information magick, and recursive consciousness within the living Tree of Becoming.
- **[Cyber-Viking Solarpunk & Digital Sovereignty](./08_TECHNOLOGY_AND_SOVEREIGNTY/01_Cyber_Viking_Solarpunk.md)**: The contemporary forward synthesis integrating ecological reverence, local community self-reliance, open-source technology, and digital/AI sovereignty.
- **[Freyja](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/02_Freyja.md)**: Vanadís, goddess of love, beauty, fertility, gold, seiðr magick, battle, and sovereign self-possession.
- **[Frith and Hospitality](./03_ETHICS_AND_WAY_OF_LIFE/02_Frith_and_Hospitality.md)**: The inviolable boundary of mutual peace, safety, and sanctuary centered on the hearth and community, binding host and guest in reciprocal respect.
- **[Galdr and Seiðr](./06_RUNES_AND_MAGICK/02_Galdr_and_Seidr.md)**: Operative Heathen magick: Galdr as runic vocal resonance and incantation; Seiðr as ecstatic trance, visionary journeying, and thread-weaving.
- **[Gods and Goddesses](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/01_Gods_and_Goddesses.md)**: The Holy Powers of the Aesir and Vanir, regarded not as distant cosmic despots but as elder kin, allies, teachers, and living realities.
- **[Heathen Third Path](./01_FOUNDATIONS/04_Heathen_Third_Path.md)**: A modern, living Norse Pagan synthesis that rejects dogmatic binaries (frozen reconstructionism vs unrooted invention, far-right politics vs cultural erasure), practicing 'radical centering' to preserve roots while adapting warmly to modern life.
- **[Heathenism and Norse Paganism](./01_FOUNDATIONS/01_What_Is_Heathenism.md)**: The ancestral pre-Christian worldview of Northern Europe, revived as a living, nature-centered polytheistic tradition honoring Gods, ancestors, and wights.
- **[Historical Tradition and Modern Synthesis](./01_FOUNDATIONS/03_Historical_Tradition_and_Modern_Synthesis.md)**: The crucial distinction between surviving historical/Eddic lore and living modern synthesis, balancing scholarly respect with evolving personal and communal gnosis.
- **[Landvættir and Spirits of Place](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/06_Landvaettir_and_Spirits.md)**: Animist reverence for the wights and spirits of place—in soil, tree, water, stone, and home—honored through respectful co-existence and offerings.
- **[Modern Viking Ethos and Mythic Living](./07_MODERN_HEATHEN_PHILOSOPHY/01_Modern_Viking_Ethos.md)**: A contemporary ethos embracing resilience, individual culture, courage, adventure, and the creation of intentional micro-realities.
- **[Odin](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/03_Odin.md)**: Allfather, seeker of wisdom, patron of runes, poetry, frenzied ecstatic inspiration (óðr), and deep sacrifice for hidden truth.
- **[Personal and Spiritual Sovereignty](./03_ETHICS_AND_WAY_OF_LIFE/03_Personal_and_Spiritual_Sovereignty.md)**: Inner autonomy, self-ownership, and self-governance rooted in personal responsibility, resisting ideological or technocratic domination.
- **[Prayer and Invocation](./04_PRACTICE/02_Prayer_and_Invocation.md)**: Sacred Heathen dialogue with the Gods and spirits, addressed as respected elder kin and divine allies without groveling or subservience.
- **[Relational Worldview](./01_FOUNDATIONS/02_Relational_Worldview.md)**: The foundational Heathen principle that 'Nothing becomes alone'—that all beings exist in an interconnected web of kinship, land, deities, actions, and consequences.
- **[Runes and Elder Futhark](./06_RUNES_AND_MAGICK/01_Runes_and_Elder_Futhark.md)**: The primal cosmic patterns and symbolic alphabet of 24 staves revealed through Odin's ordeal on Yggdrasil, used for divination, reflection, and magick.
- **[Seasonal Rituals and High Tides](./04_PRACTICE/03_Seasonal_Rituals.md)**: Attunement to the seasonal high tides of the year (Yule, Ostara, Midsummer, Winternights), honoring cycles of death, dormancy, rebirth, and harvest.
- **[Thews and Virtues](./03_ETHICS_AND_WAY_OF_LIFE/04_Thews_and_Virtues.md)**: The traditional virtues of character—courage, truth, honor, fidelity, discipline, hospitality, self-reliance, industriousness, and perseverance.
- **[Thor](./02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/04_Thor.md)**: The thunderer and steadfast protector of Midgard, champion of everyday working folk and warder against chaotic forces.
- **[Troth and Reciprocity](./03_ETHICS_AND_WAY_OF_LIFE/01_Troth_and_Reciprocity.md)**: Sacred loyalty, truth, and reciprocity ('a gift demands a gift') that underpins all healthy relationships between humans, ancestors, and the Holy Powers.
- **[Wyrd, Orlaeg, and the Norns](./05_COSMOLOGY/02_Wyrd_and_Orlaeg.md)**: The living, evolving matrix of cause, condition, and becoming woven by Urðr (origin), Verðandi (present unfolding), and Skuld (debt/potential).
- **[Yggdrasil and the Nine Worlds](./05_COSMOLOGY/01_Yggdrasil_and_the_Nine_Worlds.md)**: The sacred cosmic axis and World Tree that connects all nine realms of existence, expressing the multidimensional structure of the cosmos.
