# Prayer and Invocation

## In Brief

Sacred Heathen dialogue with the Gods and spirits, addressed as respected elder kin and divine allies without groveling or subservience.

## Core Idea

In the Heathen Third Path corpus, **Prayer and Invocation** serves as a vital structural pillar.
This concept directly builds upon foundational understandings established in [Gods and Goddesses](../02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/01_Gods_and_Goddesses.md), [Blót and Daily Practice](./01_Blot_and_Daily_Practice.md).

The corpus synthesizes this idea across 32 independent articles, drawing upon perspectives from Caducea, Volmarr.

## Key Principles

- A list of simple useful Heathen prayers to use for various situations. – Heathen House/Apartment Protection Prayer This is a simple prayer I say when leaving my apartment.
- Speak a short hymn, galdr, prayer, or spontaneous praise.
- These gods are seen as powerful elder kin – wise and mighty beings who will work with you if you approach them with respect and reciprocity. For instance, a beginner might pour out a small libation (liquid offering) to a deity and say a brief prayer of thanks.
- Many people find that talking to a deity in meditation or prayer can feel like talking to a wise mentor or beloved elder – it offers emotional support and insight.
- The act of prayer or ritual itself often involves deep breathing, calm reflection, perhaps candles and soothing atmospheres – all of which engage the parasympathetic nervous system (the body’s “rest and digest” mode).

## Relationships to Surrounding Concepts

- **Prerequisites:** [Gods and Goddesses](../02_SACRED_RELATIONSHIPS/01_Gods_and_Goddesses.md), [Blót and Daily Practice](./01_Blot_and_Daily_Practice.md)

## Distinctions

- **Dialogue vs Pleading:** Prayer is relational communion, poetry, and recognition of alliance, never self-denigrating begging.
- **Spontaneous and Formal:** Welcomes both traditional Eddic meters and heartfelt personal speech.
