# Runes and Elder Futhark

## In Brief

The primal cosmic patterns and symbolic alphabet of 24 staves revealed through Odin's ordeal on Yggdrasil, used for divination, reflection, and magick.

## Core Idea

In the Heathen Third Path corpus, **Runes and Elder Futhark** serves as a vital structural pillar.
This concept directly builds upon foundational understandings established in [Yggdrasil and the Nine Worlds](../05_COSMOLOGY/01_Yggdrasil_and_the_Nine_Worlds.md).

The corpus synthesizes this idea across 104 independent articles, drawing upon perspectives from Astrid Freyjasdottir, Caducea, Volmarr.

## Key Principles

- Primary Languages: English, the runic tongue of thought, the silent grammar of magick. > > Notable Features: No physical tattoos, but in the astral he is marked from head to toe in runes and sigils.
- If not, write the names of a few runes (like Fehu, Ansuz, or Isa) on paper slips and pick one. For example: Fehu: Abundance, what nourishes you today?
- Runes are not fortune-telling toys but mirrors of the self. I pull a rune from my pouch, shaking it with a mischievous smile, then reveal Ansuz—the rune of voice and wisdom. “See?
- For a longer esoteric rite, trace or visualize the Elder Futhark around the ritual boundary. Use the runes in order: Fehu, Uruz, Thurisaz, Ansuz, Raidho, Kenaz, Gebo, Wunjo, Hagalaz, Nauthiz, Isa, Jera, Eihwaz, Perthro, Elhaz, Sowilo, Tiwaz, Berkano, Ehwaz, Mannaz, Laguz, Ingwaz, Dagaz, Othala. Speak each name once while imagining the runic ring forming around the rite.
- No discussion of Norse Pagan practice is complete without mentioning the runes. The runes are the ancient alphabets (such as the Elder Futhark) used by Germanic peoples.
