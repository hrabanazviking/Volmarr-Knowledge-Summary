Hail Freyja, goddess of love and war,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of love and passion,  
that I may experience the joys of deep connection with others.

Bless me with the gift of fertility and growth,  
that I may be fruitful and prosperous in all areas of my life.

Bless me with the gift of beauty and charm,  
that I may attract others to me with grace and charisma.

Bless me with the gift of strength and courage,  
that I may face challenges with bravery and resilience.

Bless me with the gift of magic and divination,  
that I may harness the power of the unseen world to shape my destiny.

Bless me with the gift of wisdom and intuition,  
that I may see the truth and make wise decisions.

Hail Freyja, goddess of love and war,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
