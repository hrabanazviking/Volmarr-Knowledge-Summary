Hail Freyr, Lord of Fertility and god of abundance,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of fertility and growth,  
that I may be prosperous and fruitful in all areas of my life.

Bless me with the gift of peace and harmony,  
that I may live in a world free from conflict and strife.

Bless me with the gift of generosity and hospitality,  
that I may welcome others into my home and share my abundance with them.

Bless me with the gift of joy and celebration,  
that I may revel in the pleasures of life and enjoy the company of others.

Bless me with the gift of protection and defense,  
that I may safeguard myself and those I love from harm and danger.

Bless me with the gift of love and companionship,  
that I may find a true partner with whom I can share my life.

Hail Freyr, Lord of Fertility and god of abundance,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
