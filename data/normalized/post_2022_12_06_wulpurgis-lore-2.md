In Norse mythology and spirituality, the seasons are divided into two: winter and summer. The time between these two seasons is a time of change and transformation, known as a “border state”. This time of year is traditionally associated with witches and wizards, who use their focus on border states to bring about change and transformation. Seidh, a form of Norse witchcraft, is often performed in a trance state, which is an altered state of consciousness. Battle, sex, and other activities can also be considered border states and can be used as tools for practicing seidh and other forms of magick.

Fire is the energy of change, and the goddess Freyja is associated with fire. By attuning ourselves to the natural cycle of the seasons and practicing devotional rituals to the gods and goddesses, we can increase our own life force and magickal abilities. It is important to maintain a positive spiritual state through daily practices and limiting negative influences in order to make spiritual progress and become closer to the gods and goddesses. Negative thinking, negative energy, unhealthy habits, and lack of faith can all reduce our spiritual energy and pull us away from the spiritual path. It is important to learn how to shield ourselves against these negative influences and maintain a regular daily practice to keep our spiritual energy strong. By increasing our personal consciousness frequency and rising to a higher state of consciousness, we can attract the interest of the gods and goddesses and become closer to them.
