Hail Eir, goddess of healing and medicine,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of physical health and vitality,  
that I may enjoy the fullness of life and be able to pursue my goals.

Bless me with the gift of mental clarity and emotional balance,  
that I may be able to navigate the challenges of life with a clear and centered mind.

Bless me with the gift of compassion and empathy,  
that I may understand the struggles and suffering of others and be able to offer comfort and support.

Bless me with the gift of spiritual growth and transformation,  
that I may be able to explore the mysteries of existence and find meaning and purpose in my life.

Bless me with the gift of courage and perseverance,  
that I may have the strength and fortitude to face adversity and overcome it.

Bless me with the gift of connection and community,  
that I may find companionship and support in my healing journey.

Hail Eir, goddess of healing and medicine,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
