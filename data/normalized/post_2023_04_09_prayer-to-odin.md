Hail Odin, All-Father and Master of Wisdom,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of knowledge and wisdom,  
that I may learn the secrets of the universe and understand its mysteries.

Bless me with the gift of strength and courage,  
that I may face any challenge that comes my way with bravery and resilience.

Bless me with the gift of inspiration and creativity,  
that I may create great works of art and literature that will endure for generations.

Bless me with the gift of victory and success,  
that I may overcome any obstacle and achieve my goals.

Bless me with the gift of leadership and guidance,  
that I may inspire others and lead them to greatness.

Bless me with the gift of magic and sorcery,  
that I may wield the power of the gods to shape the world to my will.

Hail Odin, All-Father and Master of Wisdom,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
