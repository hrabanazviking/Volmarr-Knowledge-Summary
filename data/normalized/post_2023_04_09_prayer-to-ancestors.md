Hail to the Ancestors, those who have gone before,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of wisdom and guidance,  
that I may draw on your experiences and learn from your lessons.

Bless me with the gift of strength and resilience,  
that I may face life’s challenges with courage and determination.

Bless me with the gift of connection and community,  
that I may be part of a supportive and nurturing network of kin.

Bless me with the gift of tradition and heritage,  
that I may honor and preserve the customs and beliefs of my forebears.

Bless me with the gift of remembrance and commemoration,  
that I may honor and celebrate the lives of those who have passed on.

Bless me with the gift of continuity and legacy,  
that I may leave a positive and enduring mark on the world for future generations.

Hail to the Ancestors, those who have gone before,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
