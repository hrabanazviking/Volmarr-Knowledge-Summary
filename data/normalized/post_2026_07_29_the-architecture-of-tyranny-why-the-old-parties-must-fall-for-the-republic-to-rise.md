By Tess Wilder

---

We stand at a precipice in American history, gazing into the abyss of a constitutional crisis that has been brewing not for months, but for years. To call the current trajectory of the Trump administration anything other than a “slow coup” is to ignore the glaring, screaming evidence of a systematic dismantling of our democratic norms. From the violent desperation of January 6th—failed attempt number one at a fast coup—to the current, chilling methodicality of federal interference in the 2026 midterms, the pattern is undeniable.

We are witnessing a “rolling coup,” a strategic and incremental hijacking of the electoral machinery that is designed to ensure that the transfer of power becomes a mathematical impossibility rather than a civic certainty. Lawmakers, legal scholars, and veteran analysts are shouting from the rafters that the very safeguards intended to protect our vote are being turned against us. The administration has made election denialism an official federal policy, using the weight of the Justice Department and intelligence communities to manufacture fraud where none exists, all while dismantling the agencies actually tasked with protecting the ballot.

![](https://volmarrsheathenism.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/venice-studio-f27a2008-4c75-42ff-8903-0bd0f7cfc63f.png?w=1024)

Consider the implications. For the first time in modern history, the machinery of the federal government is being explicitly weaponized to override the Constitution’s clear delegation of election authority to the states. This is not politics as usual; it is a hostile takeover of the public trust by those who view the law not as a constraint, but as an obstacle to be bulldozed. When a leader attempts to federalize elections through executive fiat—ignoring the checks and balances that define our republic—we are no longer debating policy; we are debating the survival of the system itself.

> **This brings us to the uncomfortable truth that neither of our major political parties has the moral standing to save us.**

* **The Republican Party** has largely devolved into a vehicle for this authoritarian project, shielding the architect of this insolvency from accountability and actively participating in the erosion of election integrity. But the failure is bipartisan.
* **The Democratic establishment**, while sounding the alarm now, failed to ensure that the consequences for the 2020 insurrection were severe enough to deter a sequel. They allowed the precedent of an attempted overthrow to fester, treating a clear and present danger to the Constitution as a mere partisan squabble to be managed rather than an existential threat to be extinguished.

Allowing individuals who actively plot to overturn the will of the people to remain in positions of power is the single greatest act of political criminal stupidity a nation can commit. It is a violation of the social contract. It renders the concept of “loyal opposition” moot when the opposition seeks to make the opposition illegal.

![](https://volmarrsheathenism.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/venice-studio-4c809e6d-6a62-49c8-b8a4-9740f9aa272b.png?w=1024)

Therefore, the only viable path forward, the only way to salvage the American experiment from the wreckage of partisan tribalism, is a radical reset. We must face the hard reality that the current two-party system is the virus that has weakened the host body of our democracy.

We need a complete and full removal of the current federal political class. The Congress is compromised; the oversight mechanisms have failed; the loyalty to party has superseded the oath to the Constitution. We must clear the deck.

We must move to a system of full and complete non-partisan elections for the entire federal government. The era of the political party—these private clubs that have hijacked public office—must end. Political parties should be outlawed from the US political system. Let candidates stand on their own merits, their own record, and their own vision, shielded by the anonymity of the ballot rather than propped up by the war chests and tribal banners of a DNC or RNC.

This is the Cyber-Viking imperative: sever the head of the beast. Do not patch the leaks in a rotten hull; build a new vessel. The Constitution is strong, but it requires a citizenry and a government that respects its spirit over their ambition. Both parties have proven they are incapable of that respect. It is time to retire them, permanently, and return the government to the people—raw, unfiltered, and free from the tyranny of the red and blue banners.
