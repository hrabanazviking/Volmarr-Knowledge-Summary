Hail Heimdall, guardian of the Bifrost and watcher of the nine worlds,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of vigilance and awareness,  
that I may be attuned to the world around me and alert to potential dangers.

Bless me with the gift of perception and intuition,  
that I may see the hidden truths and mysteries that lie beneath the surface.

Bless me with the gift of protection and security,  
that I may be safe and secure in my home and travels.

Bless me with the gift of guidance and direction,  
that I may find my path and stay on course even in the midst of chaos and confusion.

Bless me with the gift of clarity and truth,  
that I may discern right from wrong and act with integrity and honesty.

Bless me with the gift of harmony and balance,  
that I may find equilibrium in all aspects of my life and be at peace with myself and the world around me.

Hail Heimdall, guardian of the Bifrost and watcher of the nine worlds,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
