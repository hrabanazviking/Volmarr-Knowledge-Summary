Freya, goddess of love and sensual delights   
Your beauty unmatched, your passion ignites   
In the fields of Fólkvangr you reign supreme   
Where the slain warriors come to bask in your gleam

With golden hair cascading down your back
  
And eyes that sparkle like the finest gem
  
You walk with grace and elegance divine
  
A goddess of love and sensual wine

Your charms are many, your touch is sweet   
As you dance and sway to the rhythm of your heartbeat   
In your halls of Sessrúmnir you receive   
Those who seek your company and to you they kneel

Freya, goddess of desire and lust
  
Your touch ignites the flames of passion in us
  
You teach us to embrace our sensual selves
  
To enjoy the pleasures of the flesh and its delights

Your chariot drawn by two cats you ride
  
Through the skies and across the oceans wide
  
Your love is wild and free, without bounds
  
A goddess of pleasure, beauty and sound

Freya, goddess of fertility and life
  
You bless us with abundance and joy
  
Your love is like a seed that grows and blooms
  
Bringing forth life and nourishment to all

We honor you, Freya, goddess of love
  
We thank you for the gifts you give us from above
  
May your sensual delights never fade
  
And may your love forever be ablaze.
