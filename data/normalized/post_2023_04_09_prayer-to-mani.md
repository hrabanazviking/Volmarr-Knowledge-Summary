Hail Mani, god of the moon and cycles of life,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of introspection and reflection,  
that I may delve into the depths of my own soul and uncover hidden truths.

Bless me with the gift of intuition and psychic awareness,  
that I may be able to sense the unseen and navigate the mysteries of life.

Bless me with the gift of creativity and inspiration,  
that I may be moved to create beauty and express myself in new and unique ways.

Bless me with the gift of change and transformation,  
that I may embrace the cycles of life and find growth and renewal in them.

Bless me with the gift of balance and harmony,  
that I may find equilibrium in all areas of my life and be able to achieve my goals.

Bless me with the gift of peace and serenity,  
that I may find rest and respite in the stillness of the night and recharge my spirit.

Hail Mani, god of the moon and cycles of life,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
