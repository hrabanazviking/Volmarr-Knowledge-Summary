Hail Sunna, goddess of the sun and daylight,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of warmth and light,  
that I may feel the sun’s comforting rays and be guided by its brightness.

Bless me with the gift of energy and vitality,  
that I may have the strength and vigor to face each day with purpose.

Bless me with the gift of growth and transformation,  
that I may shed old patterns and behaviors and embrace new ones.

Bless me with the gift of clarity and vision,  
that I may see the world with clear eyes and make wise decisions.

Bless me with the gift of creativity and inspiration,  
that I may be moved to express myself in new and beautiful ways.

Bless me with the gift of healing and renewal,  
that I may find the strength to overcome illness and injury and emerge whole once again.

Hail Sunna, goddess of the sun and daylight,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
