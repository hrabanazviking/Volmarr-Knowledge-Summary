Eostre, Goddess of Spring
  
Fertile, lustful, full of life
  
Your energy is wild and free
  
Bringing forth new growth and seed

Your symbols painted eggs and rabbits
  
A reminder of fertility and youth
  
Your energy is ripe and ready
  
For the start of a new season

Eostre, Goddess of Spring
  
Your beauty is unmatched
  
Your powers are strong and fierce
  
Bringing new life to the earth

Your energy is contagious
  
Spreading like wildfire
  
Bringing warmth and light
  
To the cold and dark

Eostre, Goddess of Spring
  
We honor you and your power
  
Bringing new life to the world
  
Filled with energy and vitality

Eostre, Goddess of Spring
  
We praise and celebrate you
  
Bringing forth new beginnings
  
And a renewed sense of hope.
