Hail Tyr, god of justice and courage,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of honor and integrity,  
that I may always act with honesty and fairness in all my dealings.

Bless me with the gift of courage and determination,  
that I may face my fears and challenges with bravery and strength.

Bless me with the gift of loyalty and devotion,  
that I may be steadfast in my commitments and fulfill my promises.

Bless me with the gift of wisdom and discernment,  
that I may make wise choices and judgments in all aspects of my life.

Bless me with the gift of protection and safety,  
that I may be shielded from harm and danger, both physical and spiritual.

Bless me with the gift of sacrifice and selflessness,  
that I may put the needs of others before my own and be a beacon of light for those in need.

Hail Tyr, god of justice and courage,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
