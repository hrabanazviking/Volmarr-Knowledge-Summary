Hail Njord, god of the sea and winds,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of travel and exploration,  
that I may journey far and wide with safety and ease.

Bless me with the gift of abundance and prosperity,  
that I may enjoy the fruits of my labor and share my wealth with others.

Bless me with the gift of peace and tranquility,  
that I may find calm and serenity in the midst of life’s storms.

Bless me with the gift of navigation and guidance,  
that I may find my way through the treacherous waters of life.

Bless me with the gift of protection and safety,  
that I may remain shielded from harm and danger.

Bless me with the gift of joy and laughter,  
that I may enjoy the simple pleasures of life and find happiness in all things.

Hail Njord, god of the sea and winds,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
