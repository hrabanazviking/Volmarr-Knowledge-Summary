Oh great Fehu, first rune of the first aett, I come to you with respect and reverence to ask for your blessings. Your classical meaning is that of cattle or movable wealth, which in ancient times was a symbol of status and a medium of exchange. I ask for your blessings on all aspects of wealth, including financial wealth, movable assets, and fertile land.

Please bless me with financial prosperity and help me to be responsible with the wealth that comes my way. May I not be attached to wealth, but instead be generous with my resources, so that I can create positive karma and improve my individual wyrd. May I not fall prey to greed and envy, but instead be content with what I have and appreciate the blessings that come my way.

I ask for your blessings on fertility, particularly on the fertility of livestock and crops. May I be blessed with an abundance of cattle and may they be healthy and strong. May my land be fertile and productive, so that I may have an abundance of crops and fruits.

I invoke the aid of the Vanir deities Njord, Frey, and Freyja, who are related to you on a deeper level of kenning. Please help me to extricate myself from financial difficulties and bless me with fertility and abundance in all areas of my life.

I also ask for your blessings on the creative fire that emerges from Muspelheim. May this fire inspire me to be creative and productive in my work and my life. May it help me to give birth to new ideas and new possibilities, just as Audhumla gave birth to the giants and the Aesir.

May I be blessed with a good reputation, so that I may be remembered well after I die. May I be known for my good deeds and my contributions to the world, and may my name be remembered with honor and respect.

In conclusion, oh great Fehu, I ask for your blessings on all aspects of wealth and fertility, and on the creative fire that gives birth to new possibilities. May I be responsible with my resources, generous to others, and productive in my work and my life. Thank you for your blessings. Hail Fehu!
