Eostre, goddess of spring,   
With lusty energy in full swing,   
Your fertile womb beckons forth   
The new growth of earth.

Your youth and beauty shining bright,
  
A maiden goddess in her prime,
  
Your powers of life and growth
  
Are a force to be reckoned with.

As the equinox arrives,
  
The earth awakens from its slumber,
  
And your lustful desires
  
Bring forth new life in abundance.

Eostre, goddess of spring,
  
We honor and praise your name,
  
For the abundance and growth you bring
  
To the earth and all its creatures.

With your horny energy and lifeforce,
  
We are blessed with new beginnings,
  
And we thank you, Eostre,
  
For the gift of spring.
