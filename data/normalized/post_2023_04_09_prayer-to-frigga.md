Hail Frigga, Queen of Asgard and goddess of wisdom,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of foresight and understanding,  
that I may see the world with clarity and make wise decisions.

Bless me with the gift of motherhood and family,  
that I may nurture and care for those I love with tenderness and devotion.

Bless me with the gift of weaving and crafting,  
that I may create beautiful things and express my creativity.

Bless me with the gift of protection and loyalty,  
that I may defend those who are vulnerable and remain true to my beliefs.

Bless me with the gift of magic and intuition,  
that I may harness the power of the universe to shape my destiny.

Hail Frigga, Queen of Asgard and goddess of wisdom,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
