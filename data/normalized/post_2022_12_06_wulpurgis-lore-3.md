The changing of the seasons
  
Brings a time of instability
  
Winter fades and summer grows
  
A time between, a border state

Witches and wizards thrive
  
In this time of transformation
  
Seidh magick is done
  
Through altered states of consciousness

In battle and in sex
  
In workouts and in rituals
  
Border states are fire
  
Life force energy, changing and transforming

By attuning to the cycle of the seasons
  
We increase our own life force and magick
  
Moving closer to the divine
  
We add divinity to our soul

Rising our consciousness frequency
  
We access greater knowledge and wisdom
  
And improve our spiritual luck
  
Becoming like the gods and goddesses

Shield your energies from negativity
  
And maintain a higher state of consciousness
  
Through daily spiritual practices
  
Enlightenment is an ongoing quest

Use magick to transform the self
  
And stay closer to the level of the gods
  
In this border state of change
  
We embrace the fire within.
