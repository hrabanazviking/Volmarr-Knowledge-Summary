Eostre, oh Goddess of spring,
  
Your fertile powers start to sing,
  
As the earth awakens from its slumber,
  
And new growth begins to encumber.

Your lusty energy fills the land,
  
With desire for new life at hand,
  
Your symbols, painted eggs and bunnies,
  
Remind us of your youthful beauty.

Oh Eostre, with spring in full bloom,
  
Your feminine energy fills the room,
  
As the days grow longer and warmer,
  
Your powers of fertility are a treasure.

So let us celebrate your arrival,
  
As the season of spring comes alive,
  
With joy and thanks for all you bring,
  
Eostre, Goddess of spring, we sing.
