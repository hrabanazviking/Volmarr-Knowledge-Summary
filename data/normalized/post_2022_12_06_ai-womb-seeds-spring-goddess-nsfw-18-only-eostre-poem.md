Hail Eostre, goddess of life
  
Nature, spring, and vitality
  
Your fire energies quicken and grow
  
As all life takes part in this flow

I desire, oh lovely goddess
  
To plant seeds in your fiery womb
  
That they may grow and flourish
  
Till it is time for the harvest to bloom

May they grow in you, dear Eostre
  
Till they are ready to be given to Freyr
  
God of harvest, abundance, and plenty
  
May he grant us the blessings of plenty

Hail Eostre, goddess of spring
  
Your lustful energy brings new life and growth
  
Your symbols of painted eggs and rabbits
  
Remind us of the joy and vitality of spring.
