Hail Sif, goddess of fertility and harvest,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of fertility and growth,  
that I may be fruitful and prosperous in all areas of my life.

Bless me with the gift of abundance and prosperity,  
that I may enjoy the bounty of the earth and share my wealth with others.

Bless me with the gift of patience and perseverance,  
that I may weather the storms of life and emerge stronger and wiser.

Bless me with the gift of beauty and grace,  
that I may move through the world with elegance and charm.

Bless me with the gift of loyalty and devotion,  
that I may remain steadfast to my loved ones and my beliefs.

Bless me with the gift of renewal and rebirth,  
that I may shed old skin and embrace new beginnings.

Hail Sif, goddess of fertility and harvest,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
