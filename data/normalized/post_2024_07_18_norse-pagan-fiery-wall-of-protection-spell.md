In Norse Pagan tradition, protection spells often draw upon the strength of runes, elements, and ancestral energies. Here’s a version inspired by these principles:

#### Supplies Needed:

– Red candle (for strength and protection)

– Protection runes (such as Algiz, Tiwaz, or Thurisaz)

– Salt (for purification and warding off negative energies)

– Dragon’s blood resin or oil (for empowerment and protection)

– Incense (frankincense or cedar for cleansing and spiritual connection)

– Athame or ritual knife (optional, for inscribing runes)

– Small bowl of water (to represent the cleansing and life-giving element)

#### Ritual Steps:

1. \*\*Preparation:\*\*

   – Set up your altar or ritual space with the supplies gathered.

   – Light the incense to cleanse the space and create a sacred atmosphere.

2. \*\*Casting the Circle:\*\*

   – Take the red candle and walk around your ritual space clockwise, visualizing a circle of protective energy forming around you.

   – As you walk, chant or say aloud:

     “By the runes and ancient might,

     I cast this circle strong and tight.

     Fiery wall around me rise,

     Protect from harm, under starry skies.”

3. \*\*Empowering the Candle:\*\*

   – Hold the red candle in both hands and visualize it filling with protective energy.

   – Carve or inscribe the protection runes (Algiz, Tiwaz, Thurisaz, etc.) into the candle with your athame or finger, focusing on their meanings of defense and strength.

4. \*\*Anointing with Dragon’s Blood:\*\*

   – Take a small amount of dragon’s blood oil or resin and anoint the candle from wick to base.

   – As you anoint the candle, say:

     “Dragon’s blood, fiery and bright,

     Empower this candle with protective might.

     Shield me from harm, both near and far,

     By ancestral strength and guiding star.”

5. \*\*Setting the Intention:\*\*

   – Place the candle in the center of your circle.

   – Sprinkle salt around the candle in a circle, saying:

     “Salt of Earth, protective and pure,

     Guard this space, secure and sure.

     Banish all ill, ward off all fright,

     As I will, so mote it be, this night.”

6. \*\*Invocation to Ancestors:\*\*

   – Light the candle, symbolizing the activation of your protective barrier.

   – Focus on the flame and visualize a fiery wall of protection forming around you.

   – Call upon your Norse ancestors for their guidance and protection, saying:

     “Ancient kin, wise and bold,

     Shield me now, as in days of old.

     By rune and will, by fire and ice,

     Protect this space, ward off all vice.”

7. \*\*Closing the Ritual:\*\*

   – Sit quietly within your circle, feeling the strength of the protective barrier around you.

   – When ready, thank your ancestors and the energies you’ve invoked.

   – Allow the candle to burn out completely if safe, or snuff it out with the intention of relighting it for ongoing protection needs.

8. \*\*Release the Circle:\*\*

   – Walk counterclockwise around the circle, envisioning the protective barrier gently dispersing but leaving residual protection.

   – Close with gratitude, saying:

     “By the runes and ancient might,

     I release this circle into the night.

     Protection strong, within and without,

     By my will, this spell is cast and stout.”

This Norse Pagan version of the “Fiery Wall of Protection” spell draws upon the strength of runes, ancestral connections, and elemental energies to create a powerful shield against harm and negativity. Adjust the ritual to fit your personal beliefs and practices, and always approach spellwork with respect and clear intent.
