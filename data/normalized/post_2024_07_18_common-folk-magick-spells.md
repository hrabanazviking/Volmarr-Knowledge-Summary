#### Protection Spells

1. \*\*Salt Circle\*\*: Sprinkling a circle of salt around your home or space for protection against negative energies and entities.

2. \*\*Warding Charm\*\*: Creating and hanging a charm, such as a horseshoe or a protective sigil, above your door to ward off evil spirits.

3. \*\*Egg Cleansing\*\*: Rolling an egg over your body to absorb negative energy, then disposing of the egg far from your home.

#### Love Spells

1. \*\*Honey Jar Spell\*\*: Placing a piece of paper with your and your desired person’s names in a jar of honey to sweeten your relationship.

2. \*\*Rose Petal Bath\*\*: Taking a bath with rose petals and essential oils to attract love and romance.

3. \*\*Love Knot\*\*: Tying a red string into knots while focusing on your love intentions.

#### Healing Spells

1. \*\*Herbal Sachet\*\*: Filling a small bag with healing herbs, such as lavender and chamomile, and carrying it with you or placing it under your pillow.

2. \*\*Candle Healing\*\*: Lighting a blue or green candle and focusing on the flame while visualizing healing energy.

3. \*\*Crystal Healing\*\*: Using crystals like amethyst or clear quartz and placing them on the affected area or wearing them as jewelry.

#### Prosperity Spells

1. \*\*Money Bowl\*\*: Creating a bowl filled with coins, rice, and basil, and placing it in your home to attract wealth.

2. \*\*Green Candle Spell\*\*: Carving your financial intentions into a green candle and burning it to attract money.

3. \*\*Cinnamon Stick Charm\*\*: Carrying a cinnamon stick in your wallet or purse to draw financial abundance.

#### Cleansing Spells

1. \*\*Smoke Cleansing\*\*: Burning herbs like sage or palo santo and wafting the smoke around your space to cleanse it of negative energies.

2. \*\*Moon Water\*\*: Collecting water under the full moon and using it to cleanse your space or yourself.

3. \*\*Lemon and Salt Cleanse\*\*: Placing a lemon cut in half with salt in your home to absorb negative energy.

#### Luck Spells

1. \*\*Four-Leaf Clover\*\*: Carrying a four-leaf clover for good luck and protection.

2. \*\*Rabbit’s Foot\*\*: Carrying a rabbit’s foot charm as a traditional good luck talisman.

3. \*\*Lucky Coin\*\*: Finding and keeping a coin for good luck, especially if it’s found heads up.

#### Banishing Spells

1. \*\*Black Candle Spell\*\*: Burning a black candle while visualizing the negative energy leaving your life.

2. \*\*Banishing Salt\*\*: Mixing salt with black pepper and sprinkling it around your home or space to banish negativity.

3. \*\*Mirror Spell\*\*: Using a small mirror to reflect and send away negative energy or harmful intentions.

#### Love Attraction Spells

1. \*\*Rose Quartz Charm\*\*: Carrying or wearing rose quartz to attract love and enhance self-love.

2. \*\*Apple Spell\*\*: Sharing an apple with someone you are interested in to promote love and friendship.

3. \*\*Attraction Sachet\*\*: Creating a sachet with rose petals, lavender, and a piece of your hair to attract love.

#### Fertility Spells

1. \*\*Moonstone Charm\*\*: Wearing or carrying moonstone to enhance fertility.

2. \*\*Planting Seeds\*\*: Planting seeds while focusing on fertility intentions, as a symbol of growth and new beginnings.

3. \*\*Egg Fertility Spell\*\*: Placing an egg under your bed to promote fertility.

#### Binding Spells

1. \*\*Ribbon Binding\*\*: Using a ribbon to tie around an object representing the person or situation you want to bind.

2. \*\*Poppet Spell\*\*: Creating a poppet (doll) and binding it with thread while focusing on your intention.

3. \*\*Jar Spell\*\*: Placing a representation of the person or situation in a jar and sealing it with wax.

#### Weather Spells

1. \*\*Rain Spell\*\*: Placing a bowl of water outside under the moonlight and asking for rain.

2. \*\*Sun Spell\*\*: Drawing a sun symbol and placing it in a sunny spot to attract good weather.

3. \*\*Wind Spell\*\*: Hanging wind chimes and asking for the wind to carry your intentions.

These are just a few examples of common folk magick spells that have been practiced across various cultures and traditions. Always approach these spells with respect and intention, and consider the ethical implications of your magical work.
