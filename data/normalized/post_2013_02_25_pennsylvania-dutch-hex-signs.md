[![hex sign](https://volmarrsheathenism.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/hex-sign.jpg?w=300&h=292)](https://volmarrsheathenism.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/hex-sign.jpg)Pennsylvania Dutch Hex Signs are a form of old folk magick based on runic magick, but in a form that was created by German immigrants. They are used on barns and other buildings as a way to bring in good luck, protection, and positive energies. They are talked about in the book, [Northern Magic](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1567187099/ref=as_li_qf_sp_asin_il_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=1567187099&linkCode=as2&tag=volmarr-20), by Edred Thorsson. Wikipedia has [more info](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hex_sign) on them. In Pennsylvania there survives a form of fold magick called [Pow-wow](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pow-wow_%28folk_magic%29). Many elements of Pow-wow magick hint back to older forms of northern magick, as that is it’s roots going back. You can buy hex signs from a place called [Jacob Zook](http://www.hexsigns.com/).
