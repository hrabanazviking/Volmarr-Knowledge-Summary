And her hair flows like a river.

And her lust is strong and wild.

Her body yearning for release.
  
She is the embodiment of spring,
  
And her energy is fierce and strong.

Her symbols are the painted egg,
  
And the rabbit, symbols of new life.
  
She is the goddess of the equinox,
  
And her powers are unmatched.

Her body is a feast for the eyes,
  
And her beauty is unmatched.
