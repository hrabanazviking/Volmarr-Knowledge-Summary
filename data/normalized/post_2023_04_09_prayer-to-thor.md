Hail Thor, son of Odin and protector of Midgard,  
Grant me your blessings in all things that you embody.

Bless me with the gift of strength and power,  
that I may have the courage to face any challenge and the might to overcome it.

Bless me with the gift of protection and defense,  
that I may shield myself and those I love from harm and danger.

Bless me with the gift of justice and righteousness,  
that I may stand up for what is right and fight against tyranny and oppression.

Bless me with the gift of thunder and lightning,  
that I may harness the power of the storm and strike down my enemies.

Bless me with the gift of fertility and prosperity,  
that I may enjoy the bounty of the earth and be fruitful in all my endeavors.

Bless me with the gift of loyalty and honor,  
that I may remain steadfast in my commitments and always act with integrity.

Hail Thor, son of Odin and protector of Midgard,  
Grant me these blessings, that I may honor you always.
