Freya, goddess of lust and desire,
  
Your beauty and allure cannot be denied.
  
Your eyes, like fiery embers, burn bright,
  
Drawing us in with your seductive might.

Your body, a temple of temptation,
  
Curves and swells in all the right places.
  
Your touch, electric and divine,
  
Sends shivers down our spine.

Your voice, like honey, sweet and smooth,
  
Soothes us and drives us to madness.
  
Your lips, plump and inviting,
  
Call to us with a fierce passion.

Oh Freya, goddess of love and sex,
  
We worship at your altar of sensual delights.
  
Bless us with your carnal pleasures,
  
For we are your humble devotees.

We offer our bodies to your embrace,
  
As we give ourselves to your wild embrace.
  
Let us revel in your fiery embrace,
  
As we embrace the heat of your passion.

Freya, goddess of sensual delights,
  
We are yours, body and soul.
  
Lead us into the realm of ecstasy,
  
As we honor and worship you in all your might.
