#### Supplies Needed:

– Black candle (for protection and to neutralize negativity)

– Small crystal (such as clear quartz or amethyst, for clarity and calming energies)

– Slip of paper and pen (to write the neighbor’s name or a brief description)

– Salt (for purification and protection)

– A small container (such as a jar or tin)

– Optional: Protection oil (for anointing the candle)

#### Ritual Steps:

1. \*\*Preparation:\*\*

   – Find a quiet space where you can perform the ritual without interruption.

   – Gather your supplies and set them up in front of you, focusing on your intention to quiet the disturbance.

2. \*\*Writing the Intention:\*\*

   – On the slip of paper, write the name of your neighbor or a brief description related to the disturbance (e.g., “Noisy neighbor” or “Neighbor playing loud music”).

3. \*\*Charging the Crystal:\*\*

   – Hold the crystal in your hands and visualize it filling with calming and soothing energy. Focus on your intention to bring peace and quiet.

4. \*\*Assembling the Spell:\*\*

   – Place the slip of paper with the neighbor’s name inside the container.

   – Sprinkle a pinch of salt over the paper, saying:

     “Salt to purify and protect, may this disturbance be stilled.”

5. \*\*Empowering the Candle:\*\*

   – If using, anoint the black candle with protection oil from the base to the wick, visualizing it as a barrier against negativity and discord.

6. \*\*Activating the Spell:\*\*

   – Light the black candle, seeing its flame as a beacon of protection and tranquility.

   – Hold the charged crystal in your dominant hand, focusing on the disruptive noise fading away.

   – Say aloud or silently:

     “Neighbor’s noise, be stilled and calm,

     By crystal light, bring peaceful balm.

     Silence reigns, now I decree,

     Harmony restored, so mote it be.”

7. \*\*Sealing the Spell:\*\*

   – Place the charged crystal in the container alongside the slip of paper.

   – Close the container lid, symbolizing the containment and resolution of the disturbance.

8. \*\*Closing the Ritual:\*\*

   – Allow the candle to burn down safely, or snuff it out with the intention of relighting it for ongoing protection needs.

   – Express gratitude to the Norse spirits, ancestors, or deities invoked for their assistance in bringing peace.

9. \*\*Disposal:\*\*

   – Bury the contents of the container in the earth, away from your home, to symbolize the resolution of the disturbance and returning to peaceful surroundings.
