Another really great prayer written by [Amarina](http://amarinasjourney.wordpress.com/). This one is for before starting to drive your car in wintry snowy and icy conditions.

*“Lady Skadi, Mistress of ice and snow, guide and protect. Thor, Lord and Master of the winds of the Western World, guard and protect.”*
