Hear me, O Freya,   
Goddess of sensual delight,   
Of passion and desire,   
Of lust and ecstasy.

In your embrace,
  
I am consumed by fire,
  
My body ablaze
  
With the heat of your touch.

You are the embodiment
  
Of all that is feminine,
  
Of beauty and grace,
  
Of fertility and life.

Your eyes are like sapphires,
  
Your hair like golden flame,
  
Your lips are sweet honey,
  
Your voice like a siren’s song.

You are the mistress of pleasure,
  
The queen of the night,
  
The goddess of seduction,
  
The embodiment of desire.

With your touch,
  
You awaken my senses,
  
With your kiss,
  
You ignite my passion.

In your arms,
  
I am lost in bliss,
  
In your embrace,
  
I am consumed by desire.

I worship at your altar,
  
With offerings of passion and lust,
  
For you are the goddess
  
Of sensual delights and pleasures.

O Freya,
  
Grant me the gift of your touch,
  
Of your kiss,
  
Of your embrace.

Let me be yours,
  
Forever and always,
  
For I am yours,
  
Body and soul.

So hear me,
  
O Freya, Goddess of passion and desire,
  
Of lust and ecstasy,
  
And grant me the gift of your love.
